1)What is the term Lifecycle?

Componenet Lifecycle: It is the series of stages through which a Component 
passes from the beginning of its life until its death.

Here beignning for ife means created/Inserted into the DOM
known as Mounted.

death means removed from the DOM or Un-Mounted.


2)Different Phases of a Component Lifecycle!

a) MOUNTING:When an instance of a COmponent is being 
created and inserted into the DOM.

b)UPDATING: WHen a Component is being re-rendered as a result of changes done 
to its props or state.

c)UN-MOUNTING: When a Component is being removed 
from the DOM

d)ERROR HANDLING: When there is an Error during rendering.


3)What are Side-Effects?

There are actions which are not predictable because they are actions
which are perfomed with the "outside world".

Examples : 
A) Interaction with the Browser APIs. i.e. local Storage.
B) Native DOM methods (i.e. document.getElementById  ..) instead of React DOM.
C)Fetching the Data Using an API (HTTP requests)
D)Subscribing to Database services
